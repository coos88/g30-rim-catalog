G30 fitment cheat sheet

  • Bolt pattern 5×112, hub bore 66.6 mm. Most aftermarket wheels ship with a 72.6/73.1 mm bore — always fit hub-centric rings.
  • OEM 20″ reference (Style 668 M): 8J ET30 with 245/35 R20 front, 9J ET44 with 275/30 R20 rear.
  • Aftermarket sweet spot: front 8.5J ET30–35 on 245/35, rear 9.5J ET35–42 on 275/30. Square setup: 8.5J ET35 on 245/35 all round.
  • xDrive: keep front and rear rolling circumference within ~1% of each other — the 245/35 + 275/30 stagger above does exactly that.
  • Load rating: aim for ≥ 700 kg per wheel (the G30's front axle is heavy, especially the six-cylinders).
  • Hardware: M14×1.25 bolts; match the seat (cone vs. ball) to the wheel, not the car. OEM TPMS sensors carry straight over.

Buying in the Netherlands

  • APK & RDW: wheels with an ECE-R124 approval or German ABE in an OEM-like size are in practice no issue at the APK. A TÜV Gutachten in the glovebox keeps inspectors and insurers relaxed; extreme fitments or spacers can require an RDW inspection.
  • NL webshops: Giva Wheels, Velonity and Fullcartuning stock most brands here (JR, Concaver, Z-Performance, mbDesign) with fitment checks per license plate.
  • Marktplaats: the place for used OEM 668 M or premium sets — budget €150–250 extra for a refurb (straightening + powder coat) on used wheels.
  • Tires not included: a decent 245/35 + 275/30 R20 set (e.g. Goodyear F1, Michelin PS5) adds roughly €900–1.300 mounted.
  • Winter reality: 20″ + Dutch speed bumps + winter = risk. Many owners run these as a summer set and keep 18″ for winter.
